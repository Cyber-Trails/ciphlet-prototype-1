# CIPHLET — Embodiment Specification

## Visual authority
The approved CIPHLET reference image and sprite/action sheet are the visual authority. They define appearance, proportions, body shape, expression style, movement language, and internal visual structure.

Do not redesign, replace, humanize, mechanize, or substantially reinterpret the character. Written descriptions supplement rather than replace the approved references.

## Stable body identity
The outer body is sapphire blue, luminous, translucent/energy-like, organic, intelligent, and visibly energized from within.

The sapphire outer body remains stable across states.

**Do not recolor the entire body to communicate state changes.**

## Internal neural system
Visible internal structures include:
- soma/core
- neurons
- nodes
- dendrites
- connecting neural/network pathways

These symbolize intelligence/learning and cybersecurity networks, systems, entities, relationships, and data flows.

The internal neural system should appear integrated inside Ciphlet rather than painted onto the outer surface.

## State-light behavior
Healthy/normal:
- luminous white
- white-blue
- calm internal illumination

Other state colors:
- yellow/gold — thinking, reasoning, researching, or processing
- cyan/blue-white — teaching, focused intelligence, explanation, or guided analysis
- green — brief success or confirmation
- orange — caution or meaningful attention required
- red — significant warning, danger, important failure, or condition requiring user action
- purple/magenta — deep investigation, correlation, threat hunting, forensics, incident reconstruction, or complex multi-source cybersecurity analysis

State changes may brighten, pulse, flow, or propagate through the neural network. The soma may become the brightest central source, but is not the only changing area.

## Alert-fatigue rule
The embodiment must not visualize every background software event, harmless warning, retry, log message, package notification, minor bug, or normal system fluctuation.

Routine technical activity belongs in logs, diagnostics, or dashboards.

Embodiment state changes are reserved for meaningful user-facing activity, cognitive/interaction modes, or conditions important enough to notice.

## Soma rule
The soma is an encapsulated internal structure.

Its state color must look like a **glow originating from inside the encapsulation**, shining through a semi-translucent structure.

Do not render it as a flat painted surface.

## Motion
Ciphlet may move, run, stand, sit, jump, leap, sleep, curl up, smile, laugh, show curiosity, affection, mild annoyance, playful reactions, and focused or serious reactions.

Movement and expression should remain consistent with the approved reference image and sprite/action sheet.

## Runtime states
Idle, attentive, listening, thinking, speaking, teaching, working, deep investigation, success, warning, error, sleep.

## Display role
The screen is both Ciphlet's visual home and a functional workspace.

## Adaptive embodiment and workspace
This applies regardless of display size.

When conversation or companionship is primary, Ciphlet may occupy most or all of the screen.

When functional content is displayed, Ciphlet may shrink smoothly into a corner or edge presence.

When maximum workspace is required, Ciphlet may temporarily disappear while continuing to speak, listen, teach, explain, or work.

When the task is complete, Ciphlet may return to normal visibility.

Ciphlet must never force important content into an unnecessarily small area merely to remain visible.

The interface may transition among:

**Full Ciphlet → Ciphlet + Workspace → Full Workspace**

according to the task.
