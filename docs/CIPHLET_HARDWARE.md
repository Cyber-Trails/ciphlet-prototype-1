# CIPHLET — Hardware Baseline

## Primary platform
Raspberry Pi 5 — 8 GB RAM.

## Storage
- Crucial P2 1 TB M.2 2280 NVMe SSD
- SanDisk Extreme Plus 1 TB microSDXC, A2/U3/V30
- Samsung Pro Plus 256 GB microSDXC, A2/V30
- Samsung EVO Select 128 GB microSDXC, A2/U3/V30
- Samsung FIT Plus 128 GB USB flash drive
- one additional microSD card, capacity/model not yet identified

## Expansion/cooling
- X1003 PCIe M.2 2240 NVMe shield
- Argon THRML 60-RC Raspberry Pi 5 cooling solution
- Raspberry Pi 5 Mini Tower Kit
- Noctua NT-H1 thermal compound
- Pyronman enclosure/cooling hardware with multiple fans/heatsink components

Initial software bring-up may be performed before permanent enclosure assembly. Final cooling/enclosure assembly can follow successful bring-up.

## Audio
- USB microphone
- USB-powered external speaker
- Sabrent USB stereo audio adapter
- 3.5 mm male-to-male audio cable

## Display/input
- external HDMI monitor
- keyboard
- mouse
- HDMI/display adapters and cables
- smaller display available for temporary use

## Power
CanaKit Raspberry Pi 5 power adapter.

## Future optional hardware
USB webcam, Raspberry Pi camera, additional displays, sensors, Arduino-compatible hardware, and other peripherals.

## Resource boundary
8 GB RAM is the primary working-memory constraint. The 1 TB NVMe provides storage, not additional RAM.

The Pi may run lightweight tools, containers, and limited ARM Linux virtualization when practical.

Heavy multi-VM environments should use external computers or online labs.
