# Ciphlet Prototype 1

## Raspberry Pi AI Learning Companion

Ciphlet Prototype 1 is an intact, functioning Raspberry Pi 5 AI learning-companion prototype. The owner assembled the hardware, configured a Python-based runtime, validated local AI through Ollama, and tested speech recognition, spoken responses, and multi-turn conversation on the real Pi. Production-level wake reliability and interruption behavior remained engineering work rather than blanket claims of completion.

[View Prototype 1 development stages, verified achievements and takeaways](https://github.com/Cyber-Trails/CIPHER-Showcase/blob/main/docs/ciphlet-build-journey.md).

A separate Pironman-based **Prototype 2** is planned.

Ciphlet is a separate project from CIPHER. The prototype's functional demonstrations and their documented limitations should not be confused with claims of production readiness.
